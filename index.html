User Persona · StudyFlow App

Ayesha Malik

Busy university student trying to stay on top of deadlines

"I just want to see everything due this week in one place, without opening five different apps."

Demographics

  • Age 21
  • Occupation BS Computer Science student
  • Location Lahore, Pakistan
  • Income Pocket money / part-time tutoring
  • Devices Android phone, shared laptop

Bio

Ayesha is in her fifth semester and juggles five courses, group projects and a part-time tutoring job. She tracks tasks in WhatsApp messages, screenshots and sticky notes, so she often misses deadlines or forgets what she planned to study. She is comfortable with apps but has little patience for complicated setups.

Goals

  • Know exactly what is due today and this week
  • Plan study time around classes and work
  • Get reminders that actually help, not spam
  • Finish assignments early and reduce stress

Pain Points

  • Deadlines are scattered across many apps and chats
  • Planner apps feel cluttered and slow to set up
  • Unreliable internet makes cloud apps frustrating
  • Loses motivation when tasks pile up

Behaviors

  • Checks her phone 50+ times a day
  • Studies mostly in the evening and late at night
  • Prefers quick actions over typing long entries
  • Uses dark mode and big, clear buttons

Tech Comfort

What the Design Must Deliver

Simple weekly dashboard One-tap task adding Smart reminders Works offline Dark mode Mobile-first layout
